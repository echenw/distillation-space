# 04 · 空间与地理（E1–E6）+ 超自然（H1–H3）+ 时代感（I1–I4）+ 声音与批评（G1–G4）+ 冲突 X3

---

## E1 只取轮廓，不做测绘 —— ADOPT

**when**：用真实地点做虚构场景。

**F（PG6406 L116-128）**：
> "It would be a misapprehension to suppose, that, because Melrose may in general pass for Kennaquhair… that therefore an accurate or perfect local similitude is to be found in all the particulars of the picture."
> "It was not the purpose of the author to present a landscape copied from nature, but a piece of composition, in which a real scene, with which he is familiar, had afforded him some leading outlines."

**F（PG6406 L129-151）**：作者逐条对照虚构的 Glendearg 与真实的 Allen 谷——虚构溪"touching nothing in its progress that gives token of cultivation"，源头是一座孤塔；真实的 Allen 谷上游则展开为开阔地、两岸退开、有耕地，尽头不是一座而是三座 Border 塔楼废墟。**虚构地理不必与真实地理同构，且作者逐条指出分歧处**。

**P**：声明"只取轮廓"；若用真实地名，列出你在哪几处故意不同。

**when_not**：你在写纪实性旅行/地志（那不是本 Skill 范围）。
**failure_exit**：被要求"核对真实地理"时，改为"核对真实地理 + 声明只取轮廓"（见 `references/05` 对 F22 的 REVISE）。

## E2 主动劝阻按图索骥 —— ADOPT

**F（PG6406 L197-205）**：
> "I would not advise any who may be curious in localities, to spend time in looking for the fountain and holly-tree of the White Lady."

**P**：虚构的关键地标要在副文本宣告"不可访"（= A9）。

## E3 未亲历 / 未研究之地的细节密度降级 —— ADOPT（= R3）

**F（PG4966 L89-92）**：Scott 未亲历的国度（法国与比利时、瑞士、圣地、君士坦丁堡、印度）——
> "which Scott did not visit in person, and therefore did not describe with the same attention to detail as in the case of his own country"

**F（PG1377 L10-51）**：作者自陈写《The Talisman》时对东方风俗"几乎全无知"，同期作者已把东方题材写透，他自惭。

**P**：细节密度与你的掌握程度挂钩；不熟的地方**降密度**，不要靠想象补足。这是硬约束（R3），不是建议。

## E4 交界地位 —— ADOPT

**F（PG24973 L28-34）**：Oas "at the juncture of the borders of the three greatest countries"，骷髅庙最大、Mazda 神庙空荡；并交代次要之地 Uropa、Heleste、南北 Guatama。

**P**：用**地理交界**解释一座城/一个邦的战略地位与危局，比用形容词解释更有效。

---

## E5 禁忌地名 —— ADOPT（`[单源]`）

**F（PG6406 L83-95）**：Allen 谷被称作 Fairy Dean / Nameless Dean，
> "because of the supposed ill luck attached by the popular faith of ancient times, to any one who might name or allude to the race, whom our fathers distinguished as the Good Neighbours, and the Highlanders called Daoine Shie, or Men of Peace"

**P**：让**地名本身携带禁忌与信仰层积**；不同群体对同一地点有不同的称呼，也是低成本的世界构建。

---

## E6 长期无人干预的物件开局 —— ADOPT

**F（PG43076 L251-266）**：开篇写索伦托古门上的圣安东尼奥石像——
> "for centuries kept watch thereupon"

苔藓与草穗逐年在其衣褶上绣花样，小鸟在其鼻尖与冠冕上歇息；并交代他因"despair of the human brotherhood, took to preaching to the birds and the fishes"。

**P**：开篇用一个**长期无人干预的物件**（石像、界碑、老门）承载时间深度，比用年表更省字。

## H1 给超自然留自然解释 —— ADOPT

**F（PG6406 L98-103）**：
> "In evidence of the actual operations of the fairy people even at this time, little pieces of calcareous matter are found in the glen after a flood, which either the labours of those tiny artists, or the eddies of the brook among the stones, have formed into a fantastic resemblance of cups, saucers, basins, and the like"

**P**：同一现象给出**两个成因**（小工匠 / 水流漩涡），让读者自选。不信的读者和信的读者都能读下去。

---

## H2 超自然是"已知失效仍要用"的 machinery —— ADOPT

**F（PG6406 L260-270）**（作者自陈）：
> "Machinery remained--the introduction of the supernatural and marvellous; the resort of distressed authors since the days of Horace, but whose privileges as a sanctuary have been disputed in the present age, and well-nigh exploded. The popular belief no longer allows the possibility of existence to the race of mysterious beings…"

**含义**（H）：作者清楚这套装置**已经失效**，仍要用——因此必须给他一个当代读者能接受的托底（H1 或 H3）。

---

## H3 超自然作为文化内的因果机制 —— ADOPT

**F（PG1711 L213-217）**：
> "it is quite in accordance with Zulu beliefs that a man should be haunted by the ghost of one whom he has murdered or betrayed, or, to be more accurate, that the spirit (_umoya_) should enter into the slayer and drive him mad. Or, in such a case, that spirit might bring misfortune upon him, his family, or his tribe."

**P**：把鬼魂/诅咒写成**人物信仰体系内的因果机制**，不是作者的迷信设定；并声明它在体系内成立（见 F5 的登记义务）。

---

## 冲突 X3 —— 超自然的三种写法（不统一）

| 写法 | 证据 | 读者被要求信什么 |
|---|---|---|
| 留自然解释（H1 + H2） | PG6406 L98-103、L260-270 | 信人物（人物可以信，读者不必信） |
| 文化内因果（H3） | PG1711 L213-217 | 信世界内部的规则 |
| 器物神迹（`[单源]`） | PG24973 L139-169（婴儿自称 Mazda 借唇说话）+ L78-81（头饰即王权） | 信神迹本身 |

**处置**：**三法并存，不统一**。选哪条取决于你要读者"信人物"还是"信世界"。选定后全文一致，不要中途换。

## I1 失落感建时代 —— ADOPT

**F（PG61484 L141-152）**：
> "It is, in truth, fair, this land of Provence; but fair with the sad, subtle beauty of a dream--dream of splendour, of chivalry and daring deeds, of troubadours and noble ladies; fair with the romance of undying traditions, of Courts of Love and gallant minstrels, of King René and lovely Marguerite."

并把它比作送走子女、独守旧巢的母亲，"tending and cherishing the faded splendours of yesterday"。

**P**：用**逝去**而不是**陈列**来建立时代感。

---

## I2 衰败给可计数的支出项 —— ADOPT

**F（PG61484 L154-161）**：
> "The wild extravagance of five generations of gallant gentlemen had hopelessly impoverished the last of their line. One acre after another of the vineyards and lemon groves of old Provence were sold in order to pay the gambling debts of M. le Comte, or to purchase a new diamond necklace for Madame, his wife."

**P**：写"家道中落"要给出**走了哪几笔钱、卖给谁、换了什么**。

---

## I3 伪编年声明 —— ADOPT

**F（PG61484 L161-163）**："At the time of which this chronicle is a faithful record…"
**F（PG4966 L142-148）**（配套）：读者要的是 "some air of vraisemblance"。

**P**：叙述者可自称"忠实记录"，但这是**文体声明**，不是史料声明；走 A2 契约时不要用这句。

---

## I4 时间距离副标题 —— ADOPT（`[单源]`）

**F（PG4966 L25-31）**：书名页 "WAVERLEY / OR 'T IS SIXTY YEARS SINCE"。

**P**：用副标题明示"距今多少年"，把读者的宽容度提前买下来。

## G1 弄臣特权承载政治批评 —— ADOPT（`[单源]`）

**F（PG23675 L231-240）**：Marot 称国王"at once scholar, poet, wit, soldier--"；Jacqueline 抢断——"Soldier! … When he can not conquer Italy and regain his heritage!"；Triboulet 追问"Why not?"；答——"Because the women would conquer him!" 军政失败的原因由**最无权的角色**说出。

**F（PG23675 L221-229）**：Triboulet 下令"Put him out!"，Jacqueline 先赞"How wise are your Majesty's decrees!"，再补刀"His silence is golden; his speech was dross."

**P**：给一个"说话不会被追究"的角色，把最尖锐的判断交给他；并让他的冒犯**当场不被惩罚**（这才是特权的证据）。

---

## G2 肖像只能复制外形，道德无法入画 —— ADOPT

**F（PG23675 L154-157）**：
> "But how could they depict the meanness of soul that dwelt in that extraordinary shell? The blithesome tapestry-makers, albeit adepts in form, grace and harmony, could not touch the subjectiveness of existence."

**P**：描写外貌时可以明写"外貌写不出他的本质"，把道德留给行动去呈现。

---

## G3 章题即微型合约 —— ADOPT

**F（PG23675 L51-79）**：29 个章题全是"将要发生的一件事"——A NEST OF NINNIES、A ROYAL EAVESDROPPER、THE COURT OF LOVE、THE FLIGHT OF THE FOOL、THE SECRET OF THE JESTERS、IN THE TENT OF THE EMPEROR、A MAID OF FRANCE。

**P**：章题只承诺一件将要发生的事，不作解说，不剧透结果。

---

## G4 噪声开场建社会密度 —— ADOPT（`[单源]`）

**F（PG23675 L107-119）**：开篇即人声——"A song, sweet Jacqueline!" / "No, no--" / "Jacqueline!--Jacqueline!--"；铃铛、走调的中提琴、哄笑与笼中灰鹦鹉的尖叫一起进入第一段。

**F（PG24973 L51-53）**（反向装置）：插图说明**提前剧透结局**——
> "When the soldiers of the city Oas saw that their King had not the backbone to enforce his own decree when it hurt himself, they one and all took up stones, and they stoned King So-qi to death."

**P**：用**同时发生的多种声音**替代布景描写来开门；或用副文本（插图说明/题记）提前给出结局预告——两种都可用，但要在契约层说明你选了哪种悬念策略。
