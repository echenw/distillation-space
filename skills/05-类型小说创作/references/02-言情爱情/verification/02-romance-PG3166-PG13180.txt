915	We must return for a moment to Mary Scatcherd. She was saved from the
916	necessity of encountering her brother's wrath, for that brother was
917	under arrest for murder before he could get at her. Her immediate
918	lot, however, was a cruel one. Deep as was her cause for anger
919	against the man who had so inhumanly used her, still it was natural

923	her face to the wall, and laid herself down to die: to die a double
924	death, for herself and the fatherless babe that was now quick within
