## Conflict resolution / exit

- An unreliable narrator is not a mental-health diagnosis; plot-specific narration and social pressures can be independently true.
- SF technology PG32025/PG32353 is not an experimentally validated model of memory disorders.
- PG75230 historical medical literary commentary cannot validate current clinical conditions.
- Unequal colonial, racist, gender and economic narratives need named point-of-view ownership; do not repeat their unframed norms.
- Coverage and source hashes establish file integrity and range location only, not quality of all extractions or the effectiveness of any instruction. P5/GQ5 remains NOT_PROVEN.
